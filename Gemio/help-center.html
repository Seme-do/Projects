Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password

To reset password, go to Settings → Account → Security. Enter your current password, then type your new password and confirm it Click Update Password to save you changes.

How do I add a restaurant?

Click the Add Restaurant button on the left sidebar or go to Restaurants page and click the + Add Restaurant button. Fill in the restaurant details and click Save.

Why is my payment not going through

Payment issues can occur due to several reasons: insufficient funds, incorrect card details, or network problems. Make sure your card details are correct and your account has sufficient balance. If the problem persists contact support at support@gemio.com.

Can I edit restaurant details?

Yes! Go to the Restaurants page, click on the restaurant you want to edit, then click the Edit button. You can update the name, location, cuisine type, status and other details. Click Save Changes when done.

Step-by-step Tutorials

How to Add a Restaurant

  1. Click Restaurants in the sidebar
  2. Click Add Restaurant button
  3. Fill in the restaurant name, location and cuisine type
  4. Set the status to Active
  5. Click Save to add the restaurant

How to Manage Orders

  1. Click Reservations in the sidebar
  2. View all incoming reservations in the table
  3. Change the status to Confirmed or Cancelled
  4. Click Save Changes to update

How to Update Profile

  1. Click Settings in the sidebar
  2. Go to Account section
  3. Click the Edit button
  4. Update your name, email or phone number
  5. Click Save Changes to save
